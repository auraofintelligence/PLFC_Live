PLFC Live is a one-page description of a proposed portable digital meeting
setup for Point Lookout Fishing Club.

## Public page

- [Open the PLFC Digital Meeting System](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/PLFC_Live/)

## What the page describes

- A dedicated computer as the control point.
- Two projected displays plus a committee working screen.
- Table and roaming microphones connected through the venue audio system.
- A short bump-in, structured meeting flow and pack-down process.
- Ownership, availability and governance notes for the equipment.

The repository contains the presentation in `index.html`. Image areas are text
placeholders, not supplied photographs or evidence of a completed installation.

## Open locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step or backend is required.

## Status and boundaries

This is a system concept and operating description, not proof that the full
setup is currently available, tested, insured or approved for a venue or club
meeting. Equipment ownership, access, hybrid-meeting requirements, setup times,
electrical safety and venue permissions should be confirmed with the relevant
people before use.
